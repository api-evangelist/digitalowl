# DigitalOwl

> DigitalOwl is an AI-powered platform that transforms unstructured medical records into structured, actionable data for life insurance, property & casualty insurance, and legal case management. Its products automate medical record review, summarization, triage, and data extraction for underwriting, claims, and legal demand-package analysis. This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public DigitalOwl site; the company publishes no public developer portal or OpenAPI (the Connect Data API is sales-gated).

## Products
- [Platform](https://www.digitalowl.com/platform): Overview of the DigitalOwl AI medical-record intelligence platform.
- [View](https://www.digitalowl.com/products/view): AI-enhanced 360-degree medical summaries.
- [Triage](https://www.digitalowl.com/products/triage): AI-driven audit reports and automated case flagging.
- [Connect](https://www.digitalowl.com/products/connect): Data API that maps source documents, classifications, and medical mentions (conditions, procedures, medications) into structured layers for integration into existing systems. Access is arranged via sales; no public API reference is published.
- [Chat](https://www.digitalowl.com/products/chat): Conversational AI for medical-record inquiries (beta).
- [Workflows](https://www.digitalowl.com/products/workflows): AI-assisted decisions with automated decision trees and evidence-based recommendations.
- [Case Notes](https://www.digitalowl.com/products/case-notes): Business-specific actionable insights and demand-package analysis.
